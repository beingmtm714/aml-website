A Pittsburgh partnership

Open Guild
and BOOM
Concepts.

Open Guild runs a ten-week program that teaches working artists to use AI as a business tool, not to make the art. Participants leave having built something real, something they can open on their phone and keep improving. George has run it at Berklee for four years, at Warner Bros. Discovery, and with Dan in Mexico City.

We want to build it in Pittsburgh with BOOM.

Private — for DS Kinsel and Thomas Agnew

Why BOOM.

Artists Taking Care of Artists already does the harder half of this work. Creative audits, diverse income streams, asset inventory and cash flow management.

The dojo sessions already put people in a room with a live application and a deadline.

Ten years
Fifty exhibitions. More than $250,000 paid out in artist fees.
American Cultural Treasure
Named by The Heinz Endowments and The Ford Foundation.
Google Arts & Culture
Pittsburgh's representative.
McKnight Residency
A second year hosting the consortium through Artist Communities Alliance.
The shape of it
01
Two organizations, one program
Open Guild and BOOM build the Pittsburgh program together and both names go on it. We bring the curriculum and the teaching. BOOM brings the space, the roster, and ten years of standing in this city.
02
Designed alongside your programs
Artists Taking Care of Artists and the residency already reach the artists this is for. The ten weeks sit next to them, so someone finishing ATCA has a place to go. Your think tank material goes into the curriculum with ours.
03
A first cohort we choose together
Residency artists are the obvious place to start. They have space, stipends, and a reason to come back in week six.
04
AP3 stays as it is
Public art contracting is its own machine and it works.

Where the money comes from.

Sponsors. Free to every artist who attends, and no cost to BOOM.

Open book
The budget is open. What the program costs, what we take, agreed with you before anything is signed.
Money to the artists
A share of sponsor money goes to participants rather than production, with child care and health care as the targets.
Veto either way
Either of us can take a sponsor off the list.
The joint ask

The ask lands harder from both of us than from either.

BOOM has Pittsburgh's funders and ten years of standing in this city. Open Guild has the relationships in tech. BOOM sits six blocks from Google's Pittsburgh office, in a city where Duolingo already funds community work.
